The products

Six products, one account.

Every screen below is the real thing, photographed from a running build. Nothing here is a mock-up.

Mail

Business email on your own domain, with the things people actually miss when they leave Gmail — and the things Gmail never gave them.

  • Conversations, categories with colours, filter rules applied at delivery
  • Shared mailboxes: grants, send-as, and a switcher in the rail
  • Search across every folder and every body, with operators
  • Signatures, drafts, out-of-office replies
  • Attachments scanned for viruses; oversize ones park in Space and send as a link
  • IMAP and SMTP with app passwords, so Thunderbird and Outlook still work
Mail — inbox and reading pane

Connect

Meetings that belong to the organisation, not to whoever started them. Scheduled from the console or from the phone.

  • Live meetings first in the list, so you join the one that is happening
  • Live captions, and recordings kept for 30 days
  • Minutes by TatvaOS AI — summary, decisions, action items — for organisations that switch it on
  • TatvaOS AI is optional and off by default. When your administrator turns it on, it processes content on a third-party service outside India
  • Waiting room, host controls, mute all, meeting locks
  • Guests join without an account, from a link or a code
  • A meetings API, so an ERP or timetable system can create and list them
Connect — meetings

Calendar

Day, week, month and agenda. Recurrence and reminders. Invitations that arrive properly in Gmail and in Outlook — both directions.

  • Invitations carried in the exact form each of Gmail and Outlook needs, and their replies read back
  • A meeting link on the event, carried into the invitation
  • Reminders that go out by mail
Calendar — week

Space

Files for a person, for a team and for the whole organisation, out of the same allowance as their mail.

  • My Space, Organisation, Shared with me, Trash
  • Drag-and-drop upload with progress and cancel
  • Public links with an audience you choose
  • Save a mail attachment into Space without the bytes leaving the server
Space — my files

Core — the console

Where an administrator runs the organisation. People, domains, storage, keys and the record of who did what.

  • Add one person, or upload a spreadsheet of hundreds
  • Departments, roles, and one-action offboarding with mail forwarding
  • Prove you own your domain — four methods, pick the one you can actually do
  • Buy storage once, split it across Mail and Space yourself
  • An audit trail of every administrative action, filterable by person and product
Core — organisation dashboard

Your account

One place a person manages their own identity: photo, recovery details, two-step verification, signed-in devices and what their storage is being spent on.

  • Two-step verification with an authenticator app and recovery codes
  • Every signed-in device listed, and one click to sign out everywhere else
  • An alert by mail when a new device signs in
  • Storage broken down by product
  • Family contacts, kept apart from the organisation directory
Account

The platform underneath

The parts you only notice when they are missing.

A suite is only worth more than its parts if the parts share an identity, an allowance and a record. These are the pieces that make that true.

Live

One identity, every product

A person exists once in Core. Mail, Calendar, Connect and Space read that one record. Remove them once and they are gone from all of it.

Live

Isolated by the database

Organisations are separated by PostgreSQL row-level security, not by application code remembering to filter. It holds even when the code is wrong.

Live

Sign in to your other software

TatvaOS is an OpenID Connect provider. Your library system, your ERP or your intranet can use a TatvaOS account to sign people in.

Live

Two-step verification

Time-based codes from any authenticator app, recovery codes, a replay guard, and a challenge at sign-in that a stolen password cannot skip.

Live

Storage bought once, split by you

Buy one number for the organisation. Your administrator divides it between Mail and Space and rebalances whenever they like — no new purchase, no ticket.

Live

On the phone too

The TatvaOS One Android app is on Google Play. Mail and meetings on the phone, signed in with the same account — and it hands over to the browser for everything else.

Live

Hosted in India

Your mail, files, calendar and recordings live on our server in Mumbai, with a full audit trail of every administrative action taken on your organisation.

Build on it

Three APIs, each behind an organisation key your administrator issues and can revoke. A key can never create an administrator.

  • Mail API — POST /v1/mail/send, restricted to sender addresses you name
  • People API — so your own software can admit and remove people
  • Meetings API — a paged timetable an ERP can read and write
  • SSO applications — register a client, choose the claims, see the consent screen your people will see
  • A downloadable integration guide on the page itself
Core — SSO applications
