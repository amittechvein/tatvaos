## What shipped

Last activity in the folder was **00:39–00:42 this morning**: a branch called
`mobile/connect-v1`, then `main` was pulled down. On disk now:

- **Connect is a real screen in the phone app.** There is a meetings list and a
  meeting screen written natively — not a web page in a wrapper. You can see
  your meetings, start one now, and join one.
- **Those screens come with checks that can actually fail.** Nineteen of them,
  covering every answer the server can give: waiting room, denied, wrong
  password, meeting full, screen-share refused, microphone refused, someone
  else hanging up on you. Written the night of 9–10 September without a phone
  in hand, which is the point — it is the part that *could* be proven that night.
- **And they were calibrated.** The note in `apps/mobile/__checks__/README.md`
  says exactly which check goes red when you break which thing, and then spends
  a second section listing what these checks *cannot* prove — the consent sheet,
  the speaker, the camera flip, a second person seeing the screen. That is the
  honest version. It is the best-written check in this repository.
